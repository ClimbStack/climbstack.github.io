Core Mechanics

Features & Blocks

Peaks & Camps

Define your long-term goals as mountains and break them down into base camps. Watch the SVG mountain fill up as you progress.

Weekly Planner

Organize your week intuitively. Copy previous weeks, schedule predefined templates, or create one-off blocks. Track your progress per category.

Today's Focus

Focus only on today's tasks. Tick off blocks and see the hours automatically contribute to your mountain peaks.

Blocks & Categories

Create custom categories with custom colors and icons. Manage block templates and archive them without losing historical data.

Local-First & Settings

Light and Dark mode support. 100% offline, zero trackers. Export your entire database as JSON for complete control over your data.
